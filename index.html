Why this interaction is different

The prototype does not start by teaching smartphone literacy. It starts at the exact moment the field notes showed her handing the phone over.

  • Task-first, not menu-first
  • One instruction at a time
  • "Show me" preserves agency
  • Helper remains a safety net, not a replacement

Privacy is part of the UX

The guide deliberately stops before the MoMo PIN. It can teach where to tap, but it never shows, records, or asks the person to reveal a PIN to the system or the helper.

No pressure. What would help?

This is intentionally a soft landing. Asking for help is not treated as failure.
